Mancala · Ohvalhugondi, the Maldivian mancala

Design guide

The game borrows the folio's design language, so the two read as one body of work: the same paper and ink, type, pills and plain voice. Its dark theme is the folio's Coding band, where Mancala's story is told.

Principles

  1. A calm board and a loud turn.
  2. The screen never lies about the rules: it replays the engine's events.
  3. Seeds you can see, numbers you can trust.
  4. The folio's voice: plain, short, sentence case. Seeds, not stones.
  5. Every player can play: keys, screen readers, reduced motion.
  6. Don't: neon, glows, emoji, a third colour, shadows off dialogs.
