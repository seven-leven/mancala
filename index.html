How to play Mancala

Race to collect the most stones in your store — the big pit on your side. Player 1 owns the bottom row and the right store. Player 2 owns the top row and the left store.

  • On your turn, click one of your pits to pick up its stones.
  • Stones drop one by one into the following pits, travelling around the board (skipping your opponent's store).
  • Free turn — if the last stone lands in your own store, you go again.
  • Capture — if the last stone lands in one of your own empty pits, you grab it plus everything in the pit opposite.
  • When one side is empty the game ends and each player banks their remaining stones. Most stones wins.
