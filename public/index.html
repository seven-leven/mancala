    How to play

    Bank the most seeds

    Player 1 owns the bottom row and the store on the right. Player 2 owns the top row and the store on the left.

    • On your turn, pick one of your pits. Its seeds are sown one by one around the board, counter-clockwise, past your store but never into your opponent's.
    • Relay: if the last seed lands in a pit that already had seeds, pick them all up and keep sowing.
    • Free turn: if the last seed lands in your own store, play again.
    • Capture: if the last seed lands in an empty pit on your side, bank everything in the pit opposite. The seed that landed stays put.
    • The game ends when a side runs out of seeds. Each player banks what's left on their side, and the fuller store wins.

    Keys: 1–7 play your pits, counted from your own left. N new game, U undo, H hint, S skip, ? this help.
