    How to play

    Think ahead.
    Bank more.

    Move seeds around the board, chain extra turns, and capture your opponent's seeds. Finish with the fullest store.

    Player 1Bottom row · right store

    Player 2Top row · left store

    Your turn, in 3 steps

    1. Pick a pitChoose any pit on your side and pick up all its seeds.
    2. Sow counter-clockwiseDrop one seed into each pit after it, and into your store. Skip your opponent's store.
    3. Follow your last seedWhere it lands decides whether you relay, play again, or capture.

    Three rules to remember

    Relay

    Last seed lands in a pit that already had seeds? Pick them all up and keep sowing.

    Free turn

    Last seed lands in your own store? Play again.

    Capture

    Last seed lands in an empty pit on your side? Bank the seeds in the pit opposite. Your last seed stays where it landed.

    A word of strategy

    Going first matters. Opening with pit 1 starts a chain of free turns that wins before the other side ever moves. Choose who goes first in Settings.

    How to win

    The game ends when a side runs out of seeds, or as soon as a store holds more than half of all 98. Each player banks what's left on their side, and the fuller store wins.
